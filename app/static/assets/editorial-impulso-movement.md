## Prompt final

Use case: ads-marketing. Create a premium photorealistic panoramic 3:1 campaign image for the closing banner of the Impulso sportswear e-commerce website. Use the three input images as product references: white/gray District Lime sneaker, graphite sports duffel, black Hydrate bottle with lime loop. Preserve their recognizable construction, materials, exact colors and lightning emblems. Arrange these same three products in an elegant editorial still life on dark charcoal architectural concrete podiums, bag behind, sneaker front center, bottle standing at right, subtle lime light accent on platform edges, soft directional studio lighting with clear readable products and realistic contact shadows. Entire composition concentrated in the RIGHT 60% of frame, left 40% calm near-black charcoal negative space reserved for HTML heading. Products fully visible with generous padding at top/bottom and right edges. Sophisticated sporty streetwear brand, restrained black/graphite/white/lime palette. No people, no gym equipment, no extra products, no added typography, no slogans, no watermark, no decorative floating graphics. Wide horizontal final website banner, polished high quality advertising photograph.
